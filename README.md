Lumen Drift — a neon arcade space shooter: fly the last light-ship through ten waves of drifters, seekers and weavers, chain kills into combos, grab cells to score and repair your hull, and clear the sector to win.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
```

Then open http://localhost:8080 and play with WASD or arrow keys to fly, the mouse to aim, and hold click or Space to fire. P pauses, M mutes.
